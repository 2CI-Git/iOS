# 2CI iOS

2CI is the private community platform for the Second Circle Institute. The iOS app gives members a persistent digital layer for the trust, context, and useful connection that starts inside each cohort.

## Value Prop

Senior leaders do not need another professional network. They need a trusted room that remembers who they are, what they are working through, and who else has lived a version of the same problem.

The 2CI app is designed to make that room persistent:

- **A high-trust community feed** for human updates, resources, announcements, milestones, and real responses.
- **A member directory with context** so members can find people by function, city, cohort, and affinity instead of scrolling through generic profiles.
- **A 2CI profile layer** that captures the human operating context LinkedIn misses: current challenge, cohort identity, affinity labels, and contact preferences.
- **Cohort continuity** so the bonds formed in-person do not disappear after the session ends.
- **Low-friction onboarding** so founding members can complete their profile and reach the community quickly.

## Current App Surfaces

- Native launch screen and branded in-app splash
- Feed with full post detail, replies, and member post creation
- Directory with filter modes and rich member profile detail
- Cohort view with cohort identity, stats, and member context
- Profile view with coaching, notification preferences, and settings
- First-run onboarding flow for profile completion

## Product Principles

- Human-only community content over algorithmic noise
- Trust and usefulness over engagement theater
- Lightweight member maintenance
- Native iOS quality from the start
- Mock-data frontend progress while backend contracts are still forming

## Getting Started

1. Open `TwoCi.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
2. Select the `TwoCi` scheme.
3. Choose an iPhone simulator.
4. Build and run.

## Requirements

- Xcode 15 or newer
- iOS 17 or newer
